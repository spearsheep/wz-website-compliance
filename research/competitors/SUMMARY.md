# Juris Competitive Intelligence — One-Page Summary
**May 21, 2026**

---

## The Market Moment

This is the best possible time to launch Juris. Three things just broke in your favor:

1. **The FTC fined accessiBe $1M in April 2025** for falsely claiming their AI widget makes sites WCAG compliant. Courts now reject overlay widgets as legal protection. 22.6% of 2025 ADA lawsuits hit sites that *already had a widget installed.* SMBs that paid for overlays are now getting sued anyway — and they're furious and looking for real solutions.

2. **ADA lawsuits surged 37% in 2025** to 3,948 total annual filings, with 5,500+ projected for 2026. Every small law firm, medical practice, and financial advisor is a target regardless of size — there's no employee threshold exemption for website accessibility.

3. **The May 11, 2026 HHS healthcare deadline** just hit — any healthcare organization receiving Medicare or Medicaid must now comply with WCAG 2.1 AA. Demand from medical practices is spiking with no affordable solution available.

---

## The Competitive Gap Juris Should Own

| What Exists | Price | Problem |
|---|---|---|
| Overlay widgets (accessiBe, UserWay, AudioEye) | $49–$500/mo | Legally indefensible, FTC discredited |
| Enterprise audit firms (Deque, Level Access, TPGi) | $30K–$200K+ | Way too expensive for SMBs |
| Monitoring-only SaaS (Siteimprove, Pope Tech) | $25–$6K+/mo | No remediation — just tells you what's broken |

**The gap: No one offers a flat-fee, full-service "audit + fix + certify" product for small professional services firms at $3,000–$8,000.** This segment is actively looking for exactly this after being burned by overlays.
